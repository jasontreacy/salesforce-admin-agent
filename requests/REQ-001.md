# REQ-001 — Reps close deals without saying how we'll fulfill them

**From:** Priya Natarajan, Wholesale Operations Lead, Lakeside Outfitters
**Received:** 2026-09-29
**Priority:** Medium

Hi — we keep finding Closed Won wholesale deals where nobody wrote down whether the order ships
from our warehouse, drop-ships from the vendor, or the retailer is picking it up. Ops then has
to chase the rep. Can we add a "Fulfillment Method" field on the opportunity and make it so a rep
can't mark a deal Closed Won without filling it in? Values: Ship from Warehouse, Drop Ship from
Vendor, Store Pickup. Don't make it required on *every* opp, just when they close it.
